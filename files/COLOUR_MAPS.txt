COLOUR MAPS FOR THE BONUS PACK
==============================

The atlas prints black and white for the book interior. For the bonus pack
you want the same maps in colour.

In hawaii-atlas/atlas/config.py, replace the INK dictionary with:

INK = dict(
    land="#F7F3E8",
    land_edge="#3A4A3A",
    water="#BBD9E8",
    water_deep="#9CC6DC",
    water_edge="#7FAFC9",
    park_fill="#DCE8D2",
    park_edge="#7A9A6A",
    road="#C0392B",
    road_casing="#FFFFFF",
    dot="#2C3E50",
    label="#1A1A1A",
    label_soft="#4A5A6A",
    halo="#FFFFFF",
    frame="#3A4A3A",
)

Then:
    python run.py --all --out output_colour

Combine the PNGs into one PDF:
    python -c "from PIL import Image; import glob; \
    f=[Image.open(p).convert('RGB') for p in sorted(glob.glob('output_colour/*.png'))]; \
    f[0].save('hawaii-2027-maps-colour.pdf', save_all=True, append_images=f[1:])"

Keep a copy of the original black-and-white INK block before you overwrite it,
or the book interior maps will regenerate in colour on your next run.
